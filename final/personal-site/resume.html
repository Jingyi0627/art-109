Artist Bio

Jingyi Wang is a digital media artist who is currently completing her BFA at San José State University. She practices and explores the intersection of emotion, memory, and natural elements through light, shadow, and time-based media. Working across video installation, web-based media, and photography, she creates sensory environments that reflect moments of transition and emotional ambiguity. Her work often examines the balance between inner tension and calm, using visual rhythm, atmospheric imagery, and spatial projection to evoke subtle psychological states. Inspired by natural cycles and shifting environments.

Artist Statement

Equinox is a video installation that explores ideas of balance and transition. The work reflects on in-between states, moments of uncertainty, loneliness, and emotional change that often occur during periods of personal or cultural transition. Equinox is presented as a single-channel video projection that loops continuously. The piece was created using digital video editing and compositing tools, including Adobe After Effects and Premiere. The projection is divided into two visual halves that gradually shift and merge over time, suggesting a process of movement toward equilibrium. This visual structure reflects the experience of navigating between different emotional states or cultural environments. Using light, shadow, and abstract visual imagery, the installation creates a quiet, atmospheric space for reflection. The work invites viewers to slow down and observe subtle visual changes as the projected images move between separation and unity.

Thesis

My DMA BFA thesis focuses on creating a quiet and immersive visual experience through time-based media and installation.
