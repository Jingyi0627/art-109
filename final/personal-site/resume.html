Artist Bio

Jingyi Wang is a digital media artist who is currently completing her BFA at San José State University. She practices and explores the intersection of emotion, memory, and natural elements through light, shadow, and time-based media. Working across video installation, web-based media, and photography, she creates sensory environments that reflect moments of transition and emotional ambiguity. Her work often examines the balance between inner tension and calm, using visual rhythm, atmospheric imagery, and spatial projection to evoke subtle psychological states. Inspired by natural cycles and shifting environments.

Artist Statement

My practice explores the intersection of emotion, memory, and natural elements through light, shadow, and both time-based and interactive media. I am interested in in-between states—moments of transition, uncertainty, and emotional ambiguity that shape how we perceive ourselves and our surroundings. Working across video installation, web-based media, photography, and spatial environments, I create sensory experiences that unfold gradually over time. Rather than presenting fixed narratives, my work emphasizes subtle shifts in rhythm, atmosphere, and perception, inviting viewers to slow down and become aware of quiet psychological changes.
